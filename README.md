# My Favorite Series: Stranger Things
---
## Overview
**Stranger Things** is an American science fiction horror drama television series created by the Duffer Brothers. Set in the 1980s in the fictional town of *Hawkins, Indiana*, the plot centers around the disappearance of a young boy and the appearance of a girl with psychokinetic abilities.
---
## Key Information
* **Genre:** Sci-Fi / Horror / Drama
* **Creators:** The Duffer Brothers
* **Original Network:** Netflix
* **Release Year:** 2016 – Present
---
## Favorite Characters
1. **Eleven (El)** - A girl with telepathic and psychokinetic abilities.
2. **Dustin Henderson** - The quick-witted, loyal protector of the group.
3. **Steve Harrington** - The fan-favorite mentor and protector.
4. **Jim Hopper** - The dedicated chief of police in Hawkins.
